EMANZINI

Suppression Has A Cost.

"What happens when historical violence surfaces through the next generation?"

A short film about Secrecy, Historical Violence, and Emotional Suppression.

Synopsis

A young woman is pushed to an absolute psychological breaking point from suppressing a dark family secret.

Upon discovering that one of her family members committed a heinous act, she commits an irreversible act.

Director's Statement

Emanzini explores the psychological consequences of historical violence, family secrecy and emotional suppression.

The film examines how unresolved histories can resurface through subsequent generations, shaping relationships, identities and individual perceptions of truth.
